# Synthetic demo and legal safeguards

The GTM Intelligence Hub uses **AegisOne Security**, a **fictional** cybersecurity vendor, strictly as a demo scenario. **The working name has not been trademark cleared** and should not be used as a public-facing commercial brand until clearance by qualified counsel. Product descriptions, POV metrics and thresholds in `demo_vendor.py` are illustrative and are **not representations of real vendor performance**.

## Dataset provenance and restrictions

- Generate fictional accounts, buyers, sellers, specialists, engagement records, opportunities, technical evaluations, product outcomes and financial figures. Never ingest real employer/customer records into public demo fixtures.
- Do not copy confidential sales playbooks, internal pricing, nonpublic product roadmaps, internal win/loss intelligence, customer lists, customer-specific technical details, private evaluation scripts or copyrighted sales collateral.
- Use original text for generic validation methods, synthetic technical targets and synthetic sales-enablement examples. Do not suggest that generic criteria are proprietary or validated production standards.
- Mark UI exports and demo data as **Synthetic / Fictional**; model output must distinguish assumptions, generated signals and verified source evidence.
- Do not imply sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement by any real cybersecurity company.
- Before external release, complete trademark clearance, employer contract/invention assignment review, open-source dependency license audit and a legal/privacy review.
- Public Streamlit deployment is for **synthetic data only**. Real customer records require an approved private deployment and appropriate access controls.

This file is a project safeguard, **not a legal opinion or guarantee of noninfringement**.
